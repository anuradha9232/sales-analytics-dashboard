# Insights

What the cleaned 2024 sales data (600 orders, ₹2.25 crore total revenue) actually shows. These are the control figures — any dashboard built from the same cleaned data must reproduce them exactly.

## Headline numbers

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total revenue | ₹2,24,70,216 |
| Total orders | 600 |
| Average order value | ₹37,450 |
| Best month | November 2024 (₹29,13,017) |

## Revenue by region

| Region | Revenue | Share |
|---|---|---|
| South | ₹66,29,757 | 29.5% |
| East | ₹62,88,945 | 28.0% |
| North | ₹62,15,930 | 27.7% |
| West | ₹33,35,584 | 14.8% |

**Read:** South, East, and North are tightly bunched and together drive ~85% of revenue. **West is the clear underperformer** at roughly half the revenue of each of the others — the first place to ask "why", and the most actionable finding here.

## Revenue by category

| Category | Revenue | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Electronics | ₹1,57,76,091 | 70.2% |
| Home & Kitchen | ₹29,05,503 | 12.9% |
| Apparel | ₹16,88,488 | 7.5% |
| Beauty | ₹12,69,254 | 5.6% |
| Grocery | ₹8,30,880 | 3.7% |

**Read:** The business is heavily concentrated in **Electronics — over 70% of revenue**. That's a strength and a risk: strong performance in one category, but high exposure if that category dips. The other four categories are all small and fairly even.

## What I'd look at next

- **West region:** is it fewer orders, smaller orders, or a category-mix problem? A slicer on West answers this in one click.
- **Electronics concentration:** is the dependence growing month over month? The monthly trend line, filtered to Electronics, would show it.
- **Seasonality:** November is the peak — worth checking whether that's a festival-season effect that repeats.

These are the kinds of questions the dashboard is built to answer interactively, rather than re-deriving by hand each time.
